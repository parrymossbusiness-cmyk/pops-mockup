Our Story

Built One Corner at a Time

In 2003, Kendrick "Pop" Williams Sr. was walking through downtown Pine Bluff when a corner building caught his eye, and Pop's Barber & Beauty Shop was born. Over twenty years later, it's grown into a downtown institution with a full team of barbers and stylists, and generations of the same families still coming through the door.

"I enjoy barbering, and I felt a need for it in the community." Kendrick "Pop" Williams Sr., Owner
